# Generative AI prompts (fal.ai / Canva Magic Media / any image model)

Used for optional photo-style artwork. (The fal account had no credits when the kit was built, so the
launch graphics are hand-built vector designs. These prompts are ready when you want illustrations.)
Never put AI-generated *text* in final graphics; add text in Canva or the HTML templates.

**Recommended model on fal.ai:** `google/nano-banana-2.1` (text-to-image), 2K resolution.

1. **Hero illustration (4:5, for post backgrounds)**
   > Warm, joyful editorial illustration, flat modern style with subtle grain: a diverse group of college
   > students from many cultures playing doubles pickleball on an outdoor court at golden hour in Austin,
   > Texas, live oak trees, clear sky. Players laughing and high-fiving at the net. Palette: burnt orange
   > #BF5700, cream, deep teal #0F4C5C, ball yellow #F4E04D. Empty sky in the top third for text. No text,
   > no logos, no letters.

2. **Culture-night illustration (1:1)**
   > Flat illustration of students sharing dishes from around the world at a long picnic table next to a
   > pickleball court, string lights, paddles resting on the table, cozy evening. Same palette. No text.

3. **Logo exploration (1:1)**
   > Flat vector logo mark: a pickleball whose holes are arranged along globe latitude/longitude lines,
   > burnt orange circular badge, small teal paddle behind the ball, minimal, geometric, readable at small
   > sizes. No text, no animals.
