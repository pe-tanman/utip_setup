# UTIP brand kit

**Name:** UT International Pickleball Club · **Short:** UTIP · **Tagline:** Play. Connect. Belong.

**Logo:** `logo.svg` (badge with wordmark), `logo-icon.svg` (no text). The mark is a pickleball drawn as a
globe, with a paddle behind it. It intentionally uses **no UT trademarks** (no Longhorn, seal or UT logos),
which registered orgs can't use without approval.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sunny yellow | `#FFD23F` | **primary: every background** (deep `#FFC20E`, soft `#FFF1B5` for variety) |
| Deep teal | `#0F4C5C` | ink, highlights, buttons, numbers |
| Cream | `#FFFBEA` | cards, the ball in the logo |
| Cream | `#FFF4E6` | light backgrounds, text on dark |
| Dark teal | `#082F39` | body text |

**Type:** Inter Display Black (headlines), Inter (body). Free on Google Fonts and in Canva.

**Voice:** warm, simple English (many readers are non-native speakers), inclusive ("everyone", "you're welcome
here"), no slang that doesn't translate. Always mention "beginners welcome" and "domestic students welcome".

**Using Canva:** upload `logo.svg`, create a Brand Kit with the colours above, and recreate templates from
`exports/` if you prefer editing there. **AI imagery:** prompts in `ai-prompts.md`.
