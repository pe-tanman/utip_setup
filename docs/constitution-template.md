# Constitution of the UT International Pickleball Club (UTIP)

## Article I: Name
The name of this organization is the UT International Pickleball Club, also known as UTIP
[or: International Pickleball Club at UT Austin, if SOC requires].

## Article II: Purpose
UTIP creates a welcoming, non-religious community where international students and all students
interested in global cultures connect through weekly pickleball, social events and cultural exchange.

## Article III: Membership
1. Membership is open to all currently enrolled students at The University of Texas at Austin.
2. Membership shall not be denied on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, gender, gender
   identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, citizenship or veteran status.
3. A member is any student who has completed the membership form. There are no membership dues
   [or: dues of $__ per semester, waivable on request].
4. Faculty, staff and non-students may attend as guests but may not vote or hold office.

## Article IV: Officers
1. Officers: President, Vice President, Treasurer, Events & Socials Chair [, Marketing Chair].
2. **President:** leads the org, represents UTIP to the University, maintains HornsLink registration.
   **Vice President:** supports the President, manages membership and the Discord server.
   **Treasurer:** manages funds and keeps financial records open to members.
   **Events & Socials Chair:** plans weekly sessions, socials and cultural events; manages Luma.
3. Officers must be currently enrolled UT Austin students in good standing.

## Article V: Elections and Removal
1. Officers are elected each [April] by a simple majority of members present at an announced meeting
   (announced at least 7 days in advance via Discord and Instagram).
2. Vacancies are filled by appointment of the remaining officers until the next election.
3. An officer may be removed by a two-thirds vote of members present at an announced meeting, after
   being given the chance to respond.

## Article VI: Meetings
1. The club meets weekly for play sessions during the fall and spring semesters.
2. Quorum for votes is [10 members or 25% of members, whichever is smaller].

## Article VII: Finances
Funds are used only for club activities (equipment, court fees, events). The Treasurer reports the balance
each semester. No member is personally compensated.

## Article VIII: Advisor
[Optional unless required: name of a UT faculty/staff advisor.]

## Article IX: Amendments
This constitution may be amended by a two-thirds vote of members present at an announced meeting.

## Article X: Compliance
UTIP complies with all University of Texas at Austin rules, the Institutional Rules on Student Services
and Activities, and federal, state and local laws. UTIP is a registered student organization and is
independent of the University.
