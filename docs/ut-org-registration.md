# Registering UTIP as a UT Austin student organization

Registration is done in **HornsLink** through the Dean of Students' **Student Organization Center (SOC)**.
Rules change every year, so treat this as a checklist and confirm details on the official page:
<https://deanofstudents.utexas.edu/sa/starting.php> · questions: **soc@austin.utexas.edu**

## What you need before applying
- [ ] **At least 10 members**: use the Members tab of the Google Sheet (`exportRosterForHornsLink()`
      gives Name / EID / email).
- [ ] **A constitution**: start from `docs/constitution-template.md`.
- [ ] **Officers with names + EIDs.** Annual re-registration requires at least 3 officers on the roster;
      plan for President, Vice President, Treasurer (+ Events chair).
- [ ] An application fee may apply (a $20 fee was listed in 2024). Check the current page.
- [ ] A **name that follows UT's naming rules.** Registered orgs can't imply they are official university
      units, and UT's marks (Longhorn silhouette, UT seal and logos) can't be used without licensing approval.
      Our logo uses none of them. If SOC asks, the registered name may need to read
      "International Pickleball Club at UT Austin"; the UTIP nickname can stay.

## Steps
1. **President attends New Organization Orientation.** RSVP on HornsLink. The person who will be
   president must attend the full session to get credit.
2. **Submit the application window:** the current page lists **Jan 11 – Apr 26, 2027** for spring 2027.
   HornsLink → **Organizations** tab → **Register a New Organization** → fill in name, purpose (use the
   mission below), officers, constitution upload, member count.
3. **Wait for review** (up to ~4 weeks). Watch your email for change requests.
4. **After approval:** complete the **Safety Education** module (it attaches to your new HornsLink page),
   finish the HornsLink profile (logo, description, social links), and add officers with the right access.
5. **Every year:** do annual re-registration on time (see the "Annual Registration" page on the Dean of
   Students site), keep the officer roster current, and complete any required officer training.

**Mission statement (for the application and the constitution):**
> UT International Pickleball Club (UTIP) creates a welcoming, non-religious community where international
> students and all students interested in global cultures connect through weekly pickleball, social events
> and cultural exchange. Membership is open to all UT Austin students regardless of nationality, skill level
> or background.

## What registration unlocks
- Reserving campus spaces (incl. RecSports courts) and tabling at org fairs (huge for recruitment!)
- A HornsLink page students can find and join
- Eligibility for student-org funding and the Student Org Center resources (bank account guidance, EIN, advisors)

**Later, optionally:** RecSports **Sport Clubs** is a separate, more involved program (competitive
teams, travel funding). Start as a regular registered org; consider Sport Club status once you're established.
