# Getting started with UTIP (member guide)

Welcome! Five steps, about five minutes. (This guide is also Instagram post 3 and the bot's welcome DM.)

1. **Follow us on Instagram** at @utip_austin and turn on post + story notifications. Every event drops
   there first.
2. **Fill out the member form** (link in bio): name, UT EID, email, phone, Discord username. Say **yes**
   to text alerts if you want a text when a new event is posted (reply STOP anytime). We need the member
   list to stay a registered UT org, and we never share your info.
3. **Join our Discord** (link in bio).
   - The UTIP Bot sends you a welcome DM.
   - Go to **#verify** → press **Verify membership** → type your UT EID. This unlocks the member channels.
   - Go to **#get-roles** → pick your home region, student status and skill level.
   - Say hi in **#introductions**: where you're from, your major, and one food everyone should try.
   - New to Discord? Download the app, create an account, then tap the invite link. Your *username*
     (not display name) is what goes on the form.
4. **RSVP on Luma** for each session (link in our stories, or the calendar in bio). It helps us book
   enough courts and paddles.
5. **Show up & play.** We meet every week at CCF or Whitaker Courts. Never played? We'll teach you.
   Bring water and sneakers.

**Domestic students:** you're very welcome! We're the "International" club because the founders are
international students. The whole point is meeting people from everywhere.

Questions? DM us on Instagram or ping any @Officer on Discord.
