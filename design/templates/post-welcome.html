Why we started UTIP
We went looking for an international community at UT…
…and found almost no non-religious international student orgs.
The few that exist are often inactive, and they don’t always feel welcoming when you’re new.
So we built one.

What UTIP is
A home for international students — and anyone curious about the world.
Weekly pickleball, every skill level
Socials, food & culture nights
Friends from every corner of the globe
Zero pressure. Just show up.
