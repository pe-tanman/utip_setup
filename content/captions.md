## Post 1: Welcome / why we started (carousel, 6 slides: `post1-welcome/`)

> Welcome to UTIP, the UT International Pickleball Club! 🏓🌏
>
> When we went looking for an international student community at UT, we found almost none that weren't religious. The few that exist are often inactive, and they don't always feel welcoming when you're new here.
>
> So we built one.
>
> UTIP is a place to connect with international students, and with every student who's curious about the world, through pickleball, socials and culture nights.
>
> 🏓 We play every week at CCF or Whitaker Courts. Times are posted on Luma.
> 🌱 Never played? Perfect. Beginners are very welcome.
> 🤝 Domestic students: you're SO welcome. We're the "International" Pickleball Club because our founders are international students, not because it's only for us. If you want friends from around the world and love learning new cultures, this is your club.

> 2️⃣ Join our Discord
> 3️⃣ RSVP on Luma for this week
